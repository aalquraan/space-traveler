Northern Jordan

Irbid deserves its own chapter.

Not a footnote. Irbid is one of Jordan's official tourism destinations, with museums, cultural houses, northern archaeology and access to Umm Qais and Pella.

🏛️

Dar Al-Saraya Museum

Ottoman-era building and heritage stop in central Irbid.

📖

Beit Arar

Cultural house connected to Jordanian poet Mustafa Wahbi Al-Tal.

🏺

Pella / Tabaqat Fahl

Archaeological layers spanning ancient periods in Irbid Governorate.

🌄

Umm Qais / Gadara

Greco-Roman ruins, volcanic stone architecture and northern views.
