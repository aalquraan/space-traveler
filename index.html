01.5 — Jordan in four scenes

One country.
Four completely different moods.

Petra is only the beginning. From the Rose City to the Dead Sea, the Red Sea and Jordan’s greener north, the landscape keeps changing — and that contrast is part of the story.

South · History

Petra

The Nabataean city carved into rose-coloured sandstone — a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

West · Water

Dead Sea

Extraordinarily salty water, dramatic mountains and one of Jordan’s most distinctive landscapes.

South · Red Sea

Aqaba

Jordan’s Red Sea city — beaches, marine life, diving and a completely different colour palette.

North · Green Jordan

The North

Northern Jordan

Irbid deserves its own chapter.

Not a footnote. Irbid is one of Jordan's official tourism destinations, with museums, cultural houses, northern archaeology and access to Umm Qais and Pella.

🏛️

Dar Al-Saraya Museum

Ottoman-era building and heritage stop in central Irbid.

📖

Beit Arar

Cultural house connected to Jordanian poet Mustafa Wahbi Al-Tal.

🏺

Pella / Tabaqat Fahl

Archaeological layers spanning ancient periods in Irbid Governorate.

🌄

Umm Qais / Gadara

Greco-Roman ruins, volcanic stone architecture and northern views.
